AiMY Sales Handoff

AiMY Sales is FlairsTech's AI-native sales product, built as a clickable prototype. Five people use it, from the caller who makes 200 calls a day to the CEO. This page shows every process step by step, with a link that opens each step in the prototype and a screenshot of what you should see.

How to use this page

  • Designers: read the words, the desks and the shell, then work through the processes for the desk you are changing. Rules to keep lists the decisions that are already settled.
  • Developers: read the desks, then the developer guide, data and state and traps before touching code. Each process names the functions behind it.
  • Everyone: every process has an Open button. It opens the live prototype at the right desk and record. The prototype keeps your changes in your browser, so reset before each walk-through: press the small sliders icon at the bottom right of the prototype, then Reset to seed.
  • Screenshots were taken today from a clean seed at 1536px in light mode. Click one to see it full size. Dates in the prototype move with the real clock, so a callback "due yesterday" is always due yesterday.

The words we use

The product uses these words in its copy. Use the same ones in designs, tickets and code comments.

Desk
One person's view of the product. There are five. The URL key ?as= picks it.
Contacts
The people we approach. On a caller's desk this is the call queue; on a manager's desk it is their deals. Never "board" or "book".
Account
A company. A company that already buys from us is a customer and sits in the Accounts cut on a manager's Contacts tab.
Campaign
A push to win a goal, for example "4 new clients", in one market, selling one or more of our services. A manager owns it and callers work it.
List
A set of people found for a market. A list feeds a campaign. Every campaign has at least one.
The ladder
Where one lead stands with the caller. It is a stored field, moved by calls and by one-press buttons, and every move can be undone.
Deal stage
Where a handed-over lead stands with the manager: Not met, Scoped, Shown, Priced, then Won, Lost or Follow-up.
Touchpoint
One logged contact: a call, a meeting or a move. Everything the product reports is worked out from touchpoints.
AiMY
The assistant. It lives in the bar at the bottom of every page, answers in a card above the bar, and opens full screen as the canvas.
Brief
What AiMY tells you before a call ("Before you speak to …") or a meeting ("Before you meet …"). The meeting brief is reached from Prepare me.
Say "call"
The verb is always "call", never "ring". Write plain English: no sales slang such as "warm" or "cold" in copy.

The ladder

Not called→No answer→Callback→Answered→Meeting set→Showed up→Interested→Handed over
DeclinedWrong numberDo not call

A call never moves a lead backwards and never lifts it out of an exit; only Undo does. Showed up and Interested are things a person saw, so they are one press each on the contact page. After Handed over the lead belongs to a manager and the caller can no longer call it.

Deal stages (manager)

Not met→Scoped→Shown→Priced→WonLostFollow-up

Call outcomes

Seven outcomes, each on a number key: 1 Connected, 2 Callback, 3 No answer, 4 Gatekeeper, 5 Not interested, 6 Wrong number, 7 Do not call.

Who can do what

  • Call: Engy and Lina. Not Sherif, Marit or Rami.
  • Move a lead or a deal: Engy moves the ladder; Lina moves deal stages. Nobody else.
  • Build and run a campaign: Lina. Sherif and Marit request one; Rami writes one and assigns it to a manager.
  • Build a list: Engy, Lina, Sherif and Rami can find leads through the chat. Marit has no lists.
  • Add a lead: every desk. A lead from Sherif or Marit goes straight to a manager.
  • Join a deal: Rami and Sherif (Sherif only on his product). The meeting lands in their diary with Prepare me.
  • See costs and grades: everyone except Marit.

The shell

Every desk shares one frame. It reuses the AiMY shell from Knowledge and QA: the same topnav, design tokens and components.

  • Topnav: AiMY product switcher (AiMY, Connect, Talent, QA, Sales), the dialer (caller only), theme, bell, profile pill.
  • Rail (left): one reading card about where you are ("Awaiting you", "Reading", "Found"), the Today/diary card, Financials or Your journey, and a Console link to the Knowledge console. Below 918px the rail becomes a drawer.
  • Tabs: the page heading is the switcher. Each tab shows a count; Today shows none.
  • The bar: type a question, a name, a filter, or a sentence such as "Spoke to Amelia, she wants a demo". AiMY answers in the card above. / focuses it. The mic dictates; the round button opens voice mode (needs a real microphone).
  • Toasts: every change shows a toast with Undo. There are no confirm dialogs.
  • Back links: "Back to …" always returns to the last view you were on, with its cut and scroll.
  • Record pages: campaign ?camp=, contact ?con=, account ?acc=, list ?list=.

Caller processes BDR

All of these run on Engy's desk. Reset to seed before each one.

B1Work the queue

Goal: pick who to call next.
OpenOpen the After meeting cut
  1. Read the Today briefing. Each underlined phrase opens the set it names.
  2. Use the Start strip: Call the next one, Work the callbacks, Say what happened, Find leads.
  3. Under Contacts, pick a cut: All, Callbacks, New, No answer, Answered, After meeting. A cut is a step on the ladder, so the cuts add up to All.
  4. Each card shows the step, the campaign, who they are, AiMY's reason they are here, and the number. Press Call. Fifteen cards per page; the pager is at the bottom.
  5. On After meeting, each card asks "They showed up" or "Did not show", one press each.

You should see: 109 to call; Callbacks 5, New 69, No answer 21, Answered 14, After meeting 4. Amelia Taylor is first.

Code: homePage, queueBlock, cuts, qcard, queue (ranking), BUCKETS, startStrip, topBrief.

B2Make a call and log it

Goal: one call, from ready to logged. The call is simulated: a transcript grows a line at a time.
Open Daisy Watson
  1. Press Call on Daisy Watson. The call rail opens on the right in ready, and AiMY's brief opens: Open with, Last time, If they push back, Selling, Asking for, You can send.
  2. Optional: choose which of our numbers to call from with the From menu. AiMY reuses the number this person has seen before.
  3. Press Call from …. It shows Connecting for 1.6 seconds, then live with a timer.
  4. Press the record button and agree to the consent question. The transcript only grows while recording.
  5. Press the red hang-up button. AiMY reads the call back: "I read that as connected. Is that right?", with outcome, what they asked for, checkpoint and transcript.
  6. Correct it by typing, or press 1–7. Then Log it, or Log it and call <next>.
  7. A toast confirms the log and the ladder move, with Undo.

Keyboard: Enter always does the obvious next thing (call, place, hang up, log). 1–7 pick an outcome. S skips. J/K move between cards, O opens one. Esc closes the innermost thing.

Code: startCall, callGo, callPanel, growTranscript, endCall, callLogPropose, logCall, moveFor, callPrep; fixtures SCENARIOS, CALL_SCRIPTS; numbers TEL_LINES, telPick.

B3Call a whole page in one run

Goal: call everyone on a campaign or list page, with a wait between calls (Jira AIMY-2667).
Open campaign c4Open list ls2
  1. On a campaign page press Call all 15 at the end of the cuts row. On a list page it sits in the "Who is on it" header.
  2. The first call opens in ready. Place it, then log it as usual.
  3. The next call opens with a countdown and a draining bar. At zero it places itself. Call now skips the wait.
  4. The brief carries Skip this one and Stop the run. At the end AiMY says "That run is finished".

Where the wait comes from: default 30 seconds, 5 to 120 in steps of 5. A manager sets it on the campaign (create or Edit). A list on no campaign has its own stepper. A list on a campaign shows a locked pill: the campaign's wait wins.

Code: callAll, advance, waitStart, waitBlock, waitFor, listWaitRow, waitField, sessionSummary; stored in DELTA.wait.

Rules to keep

These are settled decisions. A change that breaks one will be sent back.

Interaction

  • No modals, pickers, choice lists or drag as the only way to do something. One-click inline controls, with a toast and Undo.
  • Every change can be undone from its toast. No confirm dialogs; a decision with three answers is drawn inline (the leave gate).
  • Atomic controls (team, wait, tier) change in place on the record. Prose (pitch, persona, notes) sits behind Edit.
  • When space runs out, the component changes form (drawer, menu, own line). Never hide a control at a breakpoint.

Visual

  • No muted text. Show state with words, weight and colour, never by fading type. Nothing below the design system's --d200 for type; 14px floor.
  • Icons are Lucide, path data copied verbatim on the 24 grid.
  • Poppins is the one face. Light and dark are both maintained.
  • Anchor width is 1536px. Check 1536 and narrower; the rail is a drawer under 918px.
  • Reuse the shell: sales.css, aimy-ds.css. Sibling components from Knowledge and QA already live in sales.css under their own prefixes; look before building.

Words

  • "Call", never "ring". "Contacts", never "board" or "book". Plain English, no sales slang.
  • An AiMY insight is one plain sentence behind the AiMY mark, using the page's own nouns.
  • Money in words a person would say aloud: "€139k of €300k".
  • Profile facts must have a public source (LinkedIn, website, register, press). No team sizes or reporting lines.

Scope

  • A surface belongs to whoever does the verb on it. A roster of scored agents is AiMY QA's; Sales links to it.
  • The client never sees our cost, our grades or named prospects. Check every state of a surface, not just the surface.
  • The CEO and stakeholder read; they do not operate. Gate each verb where it is drawn and again in its handler.
  • Follow-ups go to the bell, not onto Today.

Developer guide

What the code is

  • A browser prototype with no backend and no build step. Vanilla JS, one page.
  • index.html: the shell (topnav, rail, bar, canvas, call rail, panels) and the asset stamps.
  • assets/bdr.js (about 35,600 lines): the whole product. One IIFE in sections: helpers, vocabulary, corpus (seed()), store, derivations, URL, painting, router, boot.
  • assets/bdr.css: an appendix to sales.css, numbered by section (§104 wait, §105 video, §106 About, §107 News). Only what did not exist before.
  • assets/sales.css (shell components), assets/aimy-ds.css and aimy-ds.js (shared design system and theme toggle), assets/voice.js/voice.css (voice mode, shared with Knowledge).
  • assets/audit.js: nine static checks. devserver.py: local server. old/: the archived V3 build, still served at /old/.
  • README.md is partly stale (its top still describes a BDR-only build). Trust the code and this page.

How a page is drawn

  1. The URL is the state. parse() reads these keys into S: on, con, acc, camp, list, build, bk, bt, q, p, find, chat, as, period, by, eng, ed, lk. Defaults: on=calls, q=all, period=q, by=camp.
  2. parse() also refuses what a desk cannot read: a caller's on=deals becomes Contacts; a client's lists, notes, deals, contacts and companies are cleared; by=mgr is the CEO's only.
  3. paint() dispatches in order: con → contactPage, acc → accPage, camp → campPage, lists, notes, missed, cal (diaryPage), money (moneyPage), deals (dealsPage), camps (campsPage), else homePage.
  4. go(over, replace) pushes a new URL and repaints. cleared() resets every key except as and eng.
  5. One delegated click listener routes every [data-*] attribute (data-call, data-go, data-start, data-pickopen, data-undo and so on). Menus open with data-pickopen="<id>".
  6. Long lists are windowed (vlist): only about 30 rows exist at a time. The body carries CSS zoom, so it measures in layout pixels (offsetTop), not getBoundingClientRect.

Who is reading: the predicates

  • me(): the current desk's person. DESKS = ['engy','lina','sherif','kestrel','rami'].
  • onBook(): anyone but the caller. isMgr(): the sales manager. isWhole(): the CEO. isLine(): the stakeholder. isBuyer(): the client.
  • reads(): CEO or stakeholder. works(): the opposite; gates every verb that changes something.
  • seesCost() and seesGrade(): false only for the client. Two rules, kept separate on purpose.
  • mine(k): whether a campaign is this desk's.

Data and state

  • Seed: the corpus is regenerated from one seed (SEED = 20260904) on every load, with dates relative to today: 11 campaigns, 200 accounts, about 600 contacts, 13 lists, about 1,900 touchpoints, plus a network of LinkedIn people. Ids come from indices (p170, a22, c4, ls2, n826), so links stay valid on any day.
  • Delta: only your changes are saved, in localStorage aimy-sales-bdr:db:v1 (DELTA: contact and account patches, touchpoints, lists, made records, campaigns, calendar, chats, wait, news marks, the draft list). UI settings in aimy-sales-bdr:ui, theme in aimy-ds-theme, back trail in sessionStorage aimy-sales-bdr:trail.
  • Writes: go through patchCon, patchAcc, addTouch, campSet, then a debounced save(). reindex() rebuilds the lookups (byCamp, byMgr, byLine, byClient …).
  • Reset: prototype panel → Reset to seed, or clear both localStorage keys. "Move everything on N days to today" (catchUp) shifts saved dates.
  • Console: window.BDR exposes db, go, queue, reset, stats() and more. For example BDR.go({con:'p170'}).
  • Fixtures: money, telephony, Apollo, LinkedIn, news sources, usage figures. Calls follow a script picked by each contact's hidden fate, so a demo walked twice tells the same story.
  • Load-bearing lists: the lengths of SELLS (8 services) and CLIENTS decide how the seed deals campaigns. Adding a row re-deals every figure. Compare BDR.db.camp.map(c => [c.id, c.client]) before and after.

Record shapes

  • Campaign: id, name, client, target {n, noun}, persona, size, notes, goal, aim, pitch, sells[], objections[], resources[], from, to, owner, crew[], lists[], state (running | draft | asked | done), industry, region; at runtime by, askedAt, talk, talked, givenBy, givenWhy.
  • Contact: id, acc, name, title, phone, email, camps[], owner, checkpoint, checkpointAt, attempts, lastCallAt, next {what, due}, remember, dnc, fate, manager; at runtime joined, givenBy, back.
  • Account: id, name, domain, industry, city, country, region, size.
  • Touchpoint: id, con, camp, by, at, secs, dir, outcome, proposals[], objections[], openings[], note, lines[], next, moved [from, to]; deal moves carry outcome: 'phase'.
  • List: id, name, kind, terms, crit, has[], by, at, for, via, found.

Run, check, ship

python devserver.py 8098        # then open http://localhost:8098/
node assets/audit.js            # before every commit; read the whole output
  • Use devserver.py, not python -m http.server: it sends no-store on HTML and a year's cache on stamped assets.
  • Stamps: every asset in index.html carries ?v= (today bdr.js 1322, bdr.css 1312, the rest 1286). Bump the stamp of any file you change, from its real current value. The console logs "AiMY Sales v1322".
  • Audit: checks drawn-but-unwired and wired-but-undrawn data-*, undefined classes, unused bdr.css rules, comment and brace balance, banned patterns (native select, inline handlers, transition: all), mixed line endings, call-script fixtures, and the target sums. It exits 1 on failure.
  • Commit after every verified change, on master.
  • Deploy: pushing to master deploys the Cloudflare Worker aimy-sales in about 90 seconds. Then check the live bdr.js?v= matches index.html. If the edge serves an old file under the new stamp, bump again. A clean-folder wrangler deploy is the fallback; .assetsignore keeps .git, docs and dev files off the site.
  • Siblings: AiMY Knowledge and AiMY QA sit beside this repo and share the shell. Check them before designing a cross-cutting fix.

Traps this repo has sprung

  • Line endings: files are CRLF. Many tools flatten them and the diff hides it. Check after every write; the audit flags mixed endings.
  • Shell and backslashes: heredocs and sed can eat backslashes, leaving a regex that is valid and matches nothing. Edit regexes in an editor.
  • Never delete by computed boundary. A regex-bounded deletion once removed 9,489 lines. Commit first, delete by exact text.
  • Stale delta: saved changes layer over the seed. Clear them before any before-and-after comparison.
  • Stamp cache: a changed file without a new stamp keeps serving the old one, locally and at the edge.
  • Zoom units: rects are visual pixels and CSS lengths are not. Geometry bugs show at 1536 and up and hide on a narrow pane.
  • Token overrides: each product re-declares the shell's tokens, and light-theme rules in aimy-ds.css can flatten a colour. Verify in the running page, in both themes.
  • Rename sweeps: compare whole-word and substring counts before a blanket rename; "sprung" once became "spstep".
